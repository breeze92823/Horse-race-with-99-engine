# Horse Race

Status: converted from its copy-source, Boat Race with 99 Engine (`C:\ThreeJS\Boat-race-with-99-engine`), into a horse race: the player rides a procedural horse, races on a dirt track through farm country, and the lobby is a ranch (the second world a Western frontier town). What still carries the boat game's names on purpose: the world id `beach` (saved), and references to the user's reference shots ("the Boats reference"). Git: work on `horse-race`; the boat repos are the `boat-origin` remote with pushing disabled, and nothing is pushed until the new remotes exist.

Shared prop helpers (materials, `put`, colliders, `label`, `buildOak` + `treeCollider` (round cartoon oak, `OAK` colours), `buildCliff` (optional palette), `buildWindPump`) live in `src/propkit.js`. `src/horse.js` the player's horse (tunables in `HORSE`: sizes `dims`, default colours `look`, gaits `gait`, start / stop / turn carriage `motion`): a procedural horse with saddle, bridle, reins and stirrups, every moving part ONE vertex-coloured merged mesh (body; neck > head > two ears; three tail joints; per leg upper > lower > pastern with hoof and shoe); `setLook(look)` rebuilds it in other colours. `update(dt, speed, turn, elapsed, boost, grounded, vy)` poses it procedurally: walk / trot / gallop blended by speed (`gait.toTrot` / `toGallop`), one cycle phase advancing by `stride` m; each gait says where every hoof goes (no-slip reach x `gait.reach`, `lift`, hoof `flip`) and a two-bone IK solves the leg to put it there, so hooves stay planted while the body bobs, rocks, pitches and crouches. The body pitch / drop and the neck / head ride damped springs (`motion.spring`, under-damped so the weight rocks and settles): a push-off (`motion.launch`, from the smoothed acceleration at low speed) sits the rump down, gathers the hind legs, stretches the neck and pins the ears; a halt (`motion.halt`) braces the front legs, sits the haunches, throws the head up and sprays dust forward, and a stop from above `settleFrom` ends in a head toss and shake. Turning is measured off the horse's own world heading (so main.js passes `turn` 0 in the lobby; the race passes its lane steering): lean (`rollPerTurn`), neck and head bent into the turn, the tail trailing, and the hooves step round when turning on the spot. Standing it breathes (harder after a gallop), flicks each ear on its own and fidgets (looks round, paws, sniffs the ground, shakes, rests a hind leg, swats flies). Tail streams at speed (fluttering), a tucked-legs jump pose from `grounded` / `vy`; hoof dust (a `solid` emitter behind the hooves, `HORSE.dust`, not on lite horses) fades in with speed while grounded, bursts on a push-off or a halt and streams back faster the faster it goes. `src/player.js` moves it like a horse: on the ground it runs the way it faces (no strafing or reversing on the spot), gathering speed with a tapering push (`PHYSICS.accel`, `accelTaper`), pulling up over a few metres (`decel`), gripping out side-slip (`grip`), turning tighter standing than galloping (`turnSpeed` / `turnSpeedFast`) and easing off while the heading is behind it so it arcs round (`turnSlow`). `rider` = the saddle (`HORSE.riderY`), `seat` = the horse's back just behind the cantle (`HORSE.seatZ`), where the partner rides pillion. `main.js` puts the avatar on `horse.rider` and runs the animator with `riding: true`: the `ride` state (`ANIM.ride`: seated, thighs splayed round the barrel, hands on the reins, leaning forward with speed) replaces every other state, since the horse does the moving and jumping. `setLook(look)` takes a catalog look (horseModels.js, see Horses); `dispose()` frees a display horse or picture. `src/lighting.js` sky shader, PMREM environment, shadowed sun following the player, fog, day/night blend (N; the lamp hook `addLamp` is unused now).

## Mobile & responsive (required for every change)
The game must play on phones and tablets (portrait and landscape) as well as desktop. Treat a feature as unfinished until it works on touch and on a small screen.
- **Input**: every action needs a touch path, not just a key. Use Pointer Events (`pointerdown/move/up`, check `e.pointerType`), never mouse-only events. Layout (implemented in `src/touch.js`): left-thumb virtual joystick -> `inputState.move`, right-side drag -> `inputState.look`, on-screen buttons for jump/sprint/mode/etc., pinch -> `inputState.zoom`. Everything still feeds `inputState` in `src/input.js` so game code stays input-agnostic. Pointer lock does not exist on touch; never rely on it.
- **Detection**: decide touch UI with `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` / `navigator.maxTouchPoints`, not the user agent. Show touch controls only on coarse pointers; a device can have both.
- **Layout/UI**: HUD and menus use relative units (`%`, `vw/vh`, `dvh`, `clamp()`) and flexbox/grid, no fixed pixel layouts. Tap targets >= 44px. Respect notches with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` (viewport already has `viewport-fit=cover`). Text readable at 360px width. No hover-only interactions. Add `touch-action: none` on the canvas and game controls so the browser does not scroll/zoom.
- **Resize/orientation**: anything sized to the screen (camera aspect, renderer, HUD) must react to `resize` (covers orientation changes). Use `visualViewport` where the on-screen keyboard or browser bars matter.
- **Performance**: mobile GPUs are weak. Respect the `graphics_quality` presets in `src/config.js` (cap DPR, shadow map size, draw distance); default to a lower preset on coarse-pointer devices. Avoid per-frame allocations; keep draw calls and texture sizes low.
- **Testing**: check every UI/input change at a phone viewport (e.g. 375x812 and landscape 812x375) with touch emulation, plus desktop, before calling it done.
